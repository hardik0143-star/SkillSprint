# SkillSprint V2 — Curation Notes (6 Oct 2026)

V2 adds a stronger group of hands-on, short courses after checking current provider pages for free access, duration and credential claims.

## Newly added provider groups

- **Cisco Networking Academy** — Introduction to Cybersecurity (6h), Getting Started with Cisco Packet Tracer (2h), Introduction to Data Science (6h), Find Insights with AI (6h). Cisco pages show these as free/short learning experiences; Cisco badges are used where the course/badge evidence is available.
- **Kaggle Learn** — Intro to Programming, Python, Pandas, Intro to Machine Learning, Intermediate Machine Learning, Data Visualization, Data Cleaning, Intro to SQL, Advanced SQL and Machine Learning Explainability. Kaggle states its Learn courses are no-cost and shows an estimated “hours to earn certificate” on course pages.
- **HubSpot Academy** — Reporting Certification, Inbound Sales Certification and Revenue Operations Certification. Current Academy pages identify these as free certification courses and publish their course duration.

## Existing catalog checks strengthened in V2

- Skill India’s “Unlocking AI for Everyone” currently shows free access, 45 minutes, self-paced assessment and Certificate of Completion.
- Simplilearn SkillUp currently lists free short courses with completion certificates across AI, Excel, Power BI, business analysis, project management and cybersecurity. Direct course links were improved where reliable official pages were available.
- HubSpot product certifications that explicitly require paid Pro/Enterprise practical exercises are not used as free-certificate recommendations merely because their videos are free.

## Human review rule

The weekly checker only identifies provider changes or course-link problems. It does **not** infer that a newly discovered course still satisfies all curation rules. A curator should re-check: free learning, free credential, ≤15 hours, official URL and career value before publishing it.
